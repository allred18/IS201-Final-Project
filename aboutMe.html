Background

Hi, my name is Aidan Allred and I am from Kona, Hawaii. Growing up, I loved playing sports - especially volleyball - spending time with family and friends, and traveling. Following my high school graduation, I continued my education at BYU for 1 year before serving a full-time mission in the Korea Seoul South Mission. Now, I am back at BYU pursing a degree in Global Supply Chain Management.
